Yulong Gao高玉龙

Associate Professor of Astrophysics

Research Center of Astronomy, Qinghai University · Xining, China

I am an observational astrophysicist studying how galaxies form and evolve — from metal enrichment and gas flows in dwarf galaxies to star formation and feedback driven by supermassive black holes. My work combines integral-field spectroscopy (MUSE, MaNGA), millimetre interferometry (ALMA), and large spectroscopic surveys (DESI, LAMOST, eBOSS) to understand the interplay between gas, stars, and black holes across cosmic time.

02Research Interests

🌌

Galaxy Formation & Evolution

Dwarf galaxies, galaxy mergers and induced starbursts, assembly histories, and the resolved star-forming main sequence.

🕳️

SMBHs & AGN Feedback

Supermassive and intermediate-mass black holes, quasar outflows, and star formation suppressed or triggered in galactic nuclei.

⚗️

Chemical Enrichment

Mass–metallicity relations, metal-poor gas inflow, supernova-driven enrichment, and the chemical structure of the Milky Way.

🌠

Interstellar Medium

Molecular and ionized gas dynamics with ALMA and MUSE, outflows, dust attenuation, and star formation across scales.
